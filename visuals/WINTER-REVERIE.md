# Winter Reverie asset

winter-reverie.png is an original generated landscape made for ProMax Presenter on 23 October 2026. It is not a copy of the user's reference screenshot. The scene depicts a snow-covered mountain river and spruce forest at pink-lavender dusk. The application supplies snowfall and water-only displacement through visual-engine.js, preserving the photograph's natural colors.

Keep the source asset bundled locally. No remote image service or video codec is required during playback. Test the exact and near loop boundaries, photograph loading, fixed palette and transitions with test/reference-visuals.cjs.
